SnapStudy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

SnapStudy ("the app", "we") turns a photo of a homework question into a step-by-step explanation. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. SnapStudy is operated by Badreddine Alloul, Morocco.

Information we process

  • Question photos and typed questions. When you ask for a solution, the photo you captured or selected, or the question you typed, is sent to our server and forwarded to our AI provider so an explanation can be generated.
  • Solutions and history. Questions and the explanations returned for them are saved on your device so you can reopen them. You can erase all of it at any time with Delete History in Settings.
  • Usage counts. The number of questions you have asked today is stored on your device to apply the daily free limit.
  • Diagnostics and analytics. Google Firebase collects crash reports and basic usage events, such as the app being opened or a question being solved, to help us find and fix problems.
  • Advertising identifiers. Google AdMob may access your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not require an account.

How photos are handled

Photos are used only to answer the question in them. They are transmitted over an encrypted connection, processed to produce an explanation, and are not stored on our servers. Our AI provider may retain the request for a limited period for abuse monitoring under its own terms. Please do not photograph anything beyond the homework question itself, such as personal documents or other people.

Who we share information with

  • OpenAI, our AI provider, to generate explanations.
  • Google Firebase, for crash reporting and analytics.
  • Google AdMob, to show ads.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and your choices

SnapStudy is free and supported by ads. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before requesting personalised ads. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android system settings. Learn how Google uses advertising data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Retention

Your history and usage counts stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Crash and analytics data is retained by Google according to Firebase's retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing. Because SnapStudy keeps your content on your own device, you can exercise most of this directly through Delete History in Settings. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

Children

SnapStudy is intended for students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic between the app, our server, and our providers uses encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit what we collect so there is little to expose.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use SnapStudy after a change means you accept the updated policy.
